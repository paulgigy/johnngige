ABOUT

Business Analyst,
building a background
in design, technical
support & process discipline.

My journey into business analysis was not straight forward but from the creative industry. Here is my story. I started with a degree in Business Information Technology, then spent time as a computer lab assistant supporting staff and students with everyday technical problems — patch deployments, portal issues, getting systems to talk to each other. From there I moved into design and animation, earning a diploma and later a master's degree, and spent several years as a graphic designer working directly with clients, event planners, and marketing teams — gathering their requirements, managing timelines, and turning vague briefs into finished deliverables.

More recently, I've been working as a baker, which sounds like a detour but has sharpened the same instinct every business analyst needs: follow a process precisely, notice early when something's about to go wrong, and keep quality consistent under time pressure.

I'm now completing a Business Analysis programme to put a proper framework around skills I've been building for years. This portfolio documents that transition — real experience, honestly described, plus the projects I complete along the way.
